To install paid or premium modules from the Catalog, connect your Xabber Account. This authorizes your server to access modules you've purchased or subscribed to via Xabber Services — even if your server admin login is different (perfect for teams or multiple deployments). Once connected:
  • Licensed modules appear and install with one click in the Catalog.
  • Switch accounts anytime to use different sets of licenses.
  • Uninstall a module → the license slot frees up automatically for use elsewhere.
It's quick and secure — your Xabber Account powers the best decentralized XMPP experience without compromising control.
